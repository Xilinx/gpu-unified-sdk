A higher-detail stylized low-poly pickup truck (~6,964 vertices) released into
the public domain under CC0 1.0. Attribution is not legally required; it is
provided here as a courtesy to the original author.

Local modifications:
  - The model was distributed as glTF/GLB (no OBJ in the pack). It was converted
    to Wavefront OBJ ("car.obj") with positions, normals and UVs preserved.
  - The embedded palette atlas texture was extracted to "car_texture.png"
    (512x512 RGBA). The model's colors come from sampling this atlas via the
    UVs, which is how the truck gets its multiple colors.

This sample (meshrendersample_quaternius_pickup) is a textured variant of
meshrendersample showing a higher-detail Quaternius vehicle.
